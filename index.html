How to Play

  • Place Castle: At the start, click to place your initial castle
  • Build Walls: Click grid squares to build protective walls
  • Add Castle: Click button, then click grid to add more castles
  • Add Dam: Click button, then click a wall to create a water passage
  • Survive: Protect your castle from waves for as long as possible!
